QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful information before you enquire about a PTCL Flash Fiber connection in Multan.

Is PTCL Flash Fiber available in my area?

Availability depends on your exact street, building and nearby fiber infrastructure. Send your complete address or nearest landmark through the enquiry form so availability can be checked. An area name alone does not guarantee coverage.

How can I apply for a new connection?

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp button to share your name, contact number and complete address. Your message will open in WhatsApp for you to review and send. Any application and installation are subject to PTCL confirmation.

What are the monthly package prices?

The package prices currently displayed on this page are Rs. 2,399/month for 15 Mbps, Rs. 2,899/month for 20 Mbps and Rs. 3,499/month for 30 Mbps. Confirm current offers, taxes, installation charges and terms before applying.

How long does installation take?

Installation timing depends on address eligibility, infrastructure, equipment availability and PTCL scheduling. The installation date and any applicable charges must be confirmed by PTCL.

Are the listed areas guaranteed to have coverage?

No. The listed locations are areas for connection enquiries, not a guarantee of service. Coverage must be checked for your exact address before an order is placed.
